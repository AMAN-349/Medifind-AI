Medicine: Amoxicillin
Document type: general reference
Therapeutic category: antibiotic (penicillin class)
Verification status: sample/illustrative only - not a substitute for an official drug label
Document date: 2026-10-30

Overview:
Amoxicillin is an antibiotic used to treat certain bacterial infections. It is
available in forms such as capsules and syrups, often in strengths like 250mg
or 500mg per capsule.

Common usage notes:
Dosing and treatment duration are typically determined by a prescriber based on
the type and severity of infection. Courses are often completed fully even if
symptoms improve early, as directed by a healthcare professional.

Common side effects:
May include nausea, diarrhea, or allergic reactions. People with a known
penicillin allergy should not take this medicine without medical guidance.

Important safety note:
This document is for general educational reference only. Always follow the
dosage prescribed by a qualified healthcare professional or the instructions on
the product label. Do not use this document to self-adjust medication or stop
a prescribed antibiotic course early without medical advice.
