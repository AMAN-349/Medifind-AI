Medicine: Paracetamol (Acetaminophen)
Document type: general reference
Therapeutic category: analgesic, antipyretic
Verification status: sample/illustrative only - not a substitute for an official drug label
Document date: 2026-10-30

Overview:
Paracetamol is commonly used to relieve mild to moderate pain and to reduce fever.
It is available in forms such as tablets, syrups, and injections, often in strengths
like 500mg per tablet.

Common usage notes:
Typical adult oral dosing is often discussed in ranges such as 500mg to 1000mg,
taken every 4 to 6 hours as directed by a prescriber, not exceeding the maximum
daily limit stated on the product label or by a healthcare professional.

Common side effects:
Generally well tolerated at recommended doses. Rare side effects may include
nausea or allergic skin reactions. Overdose can cause serious liver damage.

Important safety note:
This document is for general educational reference only. Always follow the
dosage prescribed by a qualified healthcare professional or the instructions on
the product label. Do not use this document to self-adjust medication.
